# Initial visual observation, before performance measurements
Observed live 1440x1000 and 390x844 Hero, pointer, scroll/gallery/about/notes, and haoqi.design hero/intro screenshots.
Strengths: coherent luminous palette; real scale hierarchy; custom crossing ribbon; spatial gallery clearly exceeds a flat grid; real projects and honest concept labels.
Main specificity finding: hero skeleton (serif statement left, floating glyph right, short intro and pill CTA) and recurring roman/blue-italic headings are transferable to another maker or AI studio. X is recognizable but describes a letter more than Xuanyi's specific practice. Work gives specificity, but router/film concept covers take first visual priority over real evidence. H = partial, insufficient, not zero originality.
Composition: disciplined bilateral hero, few overlaps, text and object remain separate. Gallery adds depth, but sections after it return to the same pale field and thin separators. Section heights are actually varied; do not claim equal heights.
Material: normal-shaded ribbon reads as a matte blue/white strip, not a fully lit physical room. Fine strip/edge lines and weak floor anchoring visible. Need light/material relationship, not heavier shadows by default.
Motion: cannot judge from stills alone; capture continuous forward/back and intermediate states next. Do not call all easing generic without reading timing and viewing intermediate samples.
Typography: strong serif sizes and italic emphasis already exist; need authored lettering/Chinese relationship and purposeful variation, not more font families.
